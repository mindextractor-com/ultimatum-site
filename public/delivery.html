Delivery and returns

Published · 20 October 2026

The book is electronic: the files arrive by email after payment. Here is how and when, and what to do if something goes wrong.

How the files arrive

Right after payment, an email with download links for your edition’s files and your Daily Frame Pro code is sent to the address you gave at checkout. If it is not in your inbox, check the spam and promotions folders.

How long the links work

The links are valid for 7 days from the moment the email is sent. Download the files and keep them on your device.

Requesting new links

If the links have expired or the email did not arrive, write to md@marketingdark.com from the address you gave at checkout, and we will send new links.

Formats and devices

  • E-book — EPUB and PDF. EPUB opens in Apple Books, Google Play Books, ReadEra and most e-readers; on Kindle, via “Send to Kindle”. PDF opens on any computer, tablet or phone.
  • Audiobook — MP3 and M4B. MP3 plays in any player. M4B is a single file with chapters, for Apple Books and audiobook apps.
  • Daily Frame Pro code — activated at daily-frame.app.

Refunds

Full refund — if the files were not delivered or do not open and the problem is not solved within 7 days of your request.

In all other cases, no refund is given once the files have been downloaded successfully.

How to request a refund

Write to md@marketingdark.com with the email you paid with, the date of payment and what exactly is wrong. The money is returned by the same method that was used to pay.
